# Voices and Weapons Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every killer's hits sound like its weapon, Chucky talks (at Home and in fights, in character and
foul-mouthed), and the announcer gets Mortal Kombat's big calls: FIRST BLOOD, FINISH HIM, LAST CHANCE, FLAWLESS
VICTORY, COMEBACK (and the Gauntlet's bout calls, recorded now for phase 3).

**Architecture:** The sounds are generated by the existing ElevenLabs tools (new tables; the controller runs them with
the key, the tools never see a file with it) into `audio-final/`, which the app loads by name as it does today. In the
app, a pure `audio/KillerVoice.kt` maps a killer and a *beat* (idle, feed, play, attack, hit taken, ...) to its lines,
so other killers get voices later by adding a table; the fight's `TurnSounds` picks the weapon sound per attacker and
at most one killer quip per turn, `GameAudio` gains `quip` (a voice line that is dropped rather than queued when the
announcer is talking), and the announcer's new calls latch on `Screen.Fight` in the view model, where the fight's turns
already end.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, SoundPool via `GameAudio`, Python 3 tools (`tools/.venv`), ffmpeg, ElevenLabs
text-to-speech (`eleven_v4`), speech-to-text (`scribe_v2`) and sound generation, JUnit4.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-24-feel-and-depth-design.md, section F (weapon impacts, Chucky's voice, the
announcer); haptics there already handle `hit_<x>` / `heavy_<x>` by prefix.

## Global Constraints

- The ElevenLabs key is read by the tools from `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` only, passed inline on the command line by the
  controller; never written to a file, a commit, a report or a log. Tools print `redact`ed errors.
- Chucky's voice is ElevenLabs' "Angry Kid" (found by name in the library; its id goes in the tool like the announcer's).
  The lines are the spec's, verbatim, swearing included:
  Home idle ×3 "Hi, I'm Chucky. Wanna play?", "What the fuck are you lookin' at?", "Hey. HEY. I'm bored over here.";
  FEED ×2 "About fuckin' time.", "You call this food, bitch?"; PLAY ×2 "Quit it, asshole!", "Ha! Do that again!";
  fight: attack ×2 "Eat this!", "Say hi to the knife!"; HEAVY "Chucky's gonna play!"; hit taken ×2 "Son of a bitch!",
  "That all you got?"; BLOCK "Nice try, bitch."; SPECIAL "Friends till the end... bitch!"; win "Ha ha ha! Nobody
  fucks with the Chuck."; lose "This isn't over, bitch!"; miss "Hold still, dammit!".
- The announcer's new lines, in the arena voice (`tools/generate_announcer.py`, `[shouting]`, capitals): "FINISH HIM!",
  "FLAWLESS VICTORY!", "FIRST BLOOD!", "LAST CHANCE!", "COMEBACK!", "BOUT TWO!" … "BOUT SEVEN!", "FINAL BOUT!",
  "CHAMPION!".
- Weapon sounds: `hit_<pet>.wav` and `heavy_<pet>.wav` for all eight `PetId`s (16 sounds), through
  `tools/generate_game_sfx.py`'s pipeline (a new table), picked by ear; `crit` still plays instead of a hit's sound as
  today; a bleed tick stays `hit`.
- Playback rules: at Home an idle line every 25–45 s while nothing else plays (never during an action, a bubble, or
  sleep; Home only), one line per FEED (as the reaction lands) and per PLAY (at the gag's reaction); in a fight at most
  one killer line per turn, with a 50% chance, on the matching beat, never over the announcer (dropped, not queued);
  win/lose lines follow the announcer's YOU WIN / YOU LOSE in the queue.
- Secret: the eighth killer is never named in README, CREDITS, the site or release notes (his sounds are files like the
  others'; the tools' tables may name him, docs may not).
- Audio files: 16-bit PCM mono WAV, 44.1 kHz, in `audio-final/voice/` (lines) and `audio-final/sfx/` (weapons); the
  app's `GameAudio` loads everything in those folders by name, nothing to register. `GameState.version` unchanged.
- Tests pure-JVM; sound-timing checks on `emulator-5554` with `adb logcat -s GameAudio` (it logs every sfx and voice).

## Review Focus

1. A killer with no voice table (every pet but Chucky, PvP opponents included): no line, no lookup crash, the announcer
   as today — Task 3's `everyOtherKillerIsSilent`.
2. FINISH HIM when the CPU drops under 20% on the blow that also knocks it out: not said (the fight does not continue)
   — Task 5's `finishHimWaitsForAFightThatGoesOn`.
3. FIRST BLOOD: the fight's first `Hit` (a counter counts; a bleed tick does not), once, even if the player's first hit
   came after the CPU's — Task 5's `firstBloodIsTheFirstHitOfTheFight`.
4. FLAWLESS VICTORY needs a win with no damage taken at all (a potion back to full after a hit is not flawless; a run
   is no win) — Task 5's `flawlessMeansNeverScratched`.
5. A Chucky fight line and the announcer's callout in the same turn: the announcer wins, the quip is dropped, never
   two voices at once — Task 3's `theAnnouncerWinsTheTurn` (TurnSounds) and `aQuipIsDroppedWhenTheAnnouncerTalks`
   (GameAudio's rule, as a pure `VoiceQueue` check).

**Interfaces:**
- `enum class Beat { IDLE, FEED, PLAY, ATTACK, HEAVY, HURT, BLOCK, SPECIAL, WIN, LOSE, MISS }`
- `fun killerLines(pet: PetId, beat: Beat): List<String>` — Chucky's names from Task 1 per beat; `emptyList()` for
  every other pet.
- `fun beatsOf(event: FightEvent, side: Side): List<Beat>` — the beats [side]'s killer has in [event]: its own

  announcer wins; see Review Focus 5) or the event is the KO, taken with [chance]; never the same line twice running.
- `SoundSink.quip(name)`: say the line now if the announcer is idle, else drop it.
