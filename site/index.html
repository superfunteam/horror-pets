Install it on your TV

  1. On your Google TV, Onn, Fire TV or Android TV, install Downloader by AFTVnews from the app store. It's free.
  2. In your TV's settings, allow Downloader to install unknown apps.
  3. Open Downloader, go to this page's address, and press Download for Android.
  4. Open the file and install it. After that, the game offers new versions itself, and they keep your pets.

On a phone, tap Download for Android, open the file, and allow the install.
